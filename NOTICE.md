# Third-party avatar artwork

The 100 classic QQ avatar bitmaps used by this local QQ-2007-era presentation are
Tencent artwork. They are not covered by this repository's MIT code license.

The unmodified PNGs are sourced from the original-QQ resource extraction in
[mengkunsoft/QQ2006](https://github.com/mengkunsoft/QQ2006), revision
`2dce4e47beb9b55494c492a6cf6e2c01eb77dbe3`, `img/avatar/1.png` through `100.png`.
This is the inherited classic set, not a claim to include every later QQ 2007
avatar or the 2019 anniversary redesign. The source documents Tencent ownership
and learning/non-commercial use. Preserve this notice; commercial reuse requires
appropriate permission from the rights holder.

`scripts/vendor-classic-avatars.mjs` embeds these exact files in the client so
visitors do not make avatar requests to a third-party site. No QQ logo, service
integration, account identity, endorsement or affiliation is implied.
